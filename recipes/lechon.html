Lechon

The ultimate Filipino celebration food—crispy on the outside, juicy on the inside.

Lechon (roasted pig) is the centerpiece of fiestas, weddings, and holidays. While whole lechon is usually for special occasions, Lechon Kawali is a popular home-style crispy pork belly alternative.

Ingredients for Lechon Kawali:

  • 1 kg pork belly
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp peppercorns
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2-3 bay leaves
  • 2-3 bay leaves
  • Water for boiling
  • Oil for deep-frying

Steps:

  1. Boil pork belly with salt, peppercorns, garlic, and bay leaves for 45–60 minutes or until tender.
  2. Let the pork dry completely (best if chilled overnight).
  3. Deep-fry the pork until golden and crispy.
  4. Slice and serve with liver sauce (lechon sauce) or vinegar dip.
