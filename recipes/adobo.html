Adobo

The iconic dish that defines Filipino home cooking.

Every Filipino household has its own version of adobo—but the essentials remain the same: soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and pepper. It can be made with pork, chicken, or both.

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg pork belly or chicken (or both), cut into chunks
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp whole peppercorns
  • 2-3 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar (optional)
  • Water as needed
  • Salt and oil

Steps:

  1. 1. Marinate meat in soy sauce, garlic, and peppercorns for 30 minutes.
  2. 2. Place in a pot, add vinegar and bay leaves. Do not stir. Let it boil for 5 minutes.
  3. 3. Lower heat, cover, and simmer until tender (30–45 minutes).
  4. 4. Optionally fry the meat for a dry-style adobo, then return it to the sauce.
  5. 5. Serve with rice.
